## Customer Churn Prediction
A subscription-based company wants to identify customers who are likely to leave. The company wants to understand churn drivers and take action before losing valuable customers.

## Key Insights

1. Customers with month-to-month contracts showed higher churn risk.
2. Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to churn.
3. Monthly charges appeared to be an important factor in churn behaviour.
4. Contract type, tenure, and service usage were useful predictors.

## Business Recommendations

1. Offer retention incentives to high-risk month-to-month customers.
2. Encourage customers to move to longer-term contracts through loyalty benefits.
3. Monitor customers with high monthly charges and short tenure.
4. Use churn prediction scores to prioritise customer service follow-up.
